Basics
Breaks & flow
Group size

The app fills tables with groups of 4, using a few 3s so any headcount fits. Nobody is left standing.

Conversation prompts

One prompt shows on the room screen each round. In Auto mode they start light and get deeper as the night warms up.

Who's here

Add people as they arrive — walk-ins are welcome any time, even mid-event. If two people came together, put the same label in “Came with” so they won't be seated together.
